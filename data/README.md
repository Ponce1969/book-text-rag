# data — candidate source material

This directory holds the **original, unmodified** source books used to evaluate and
ingest the corpus. Nothing here is generated by the project.

## What goes here

- One file per candidate book, in its original format (PDF, EPUB, ...).
- The winning candidate becomes the pilot source for Phase 1.
- Non-winning candidates stay here until the corpus decision closes.

## What does NOT go here

- Extracted text, chunks, embeddings, or any derived artifact (use `data/extracted/`
  and `data/derived/`, both ignored by git).
- Anything produced by a pipeline run.

## Naming convention

`<author-lastname>-<short-title>.<ext>` — for example `huyen-ai-engineering.pdf`.
Avoid spaces and non-ASCII characters: they break shell paths on the host.

## Legal and size

These files are copyrighted and often large. They are **never committed**: the root
`.gitignore` excludes book formats and derived directories. Verify the licence and
that you may hold a copy before ingesting.

## Status

Candidates are inspected read-only. A book is accepted as pilot only after its text
layer, structure (chapters/sections), and page reliability are verified — see
`docs/spec/004-decisions.md` (D-01) and `docs/spec/002-mvp.md` (Phase 0).
